Another year another pit! Thank y'all so much for making this the best year yet! Without y'all, this event wouldn't be the incredible experience it is today, so thank you for taking part in this crazy adventure. It is a lot of work putting on this event, and seeing everyone together, eating good food, listening to good music, bringing the community spirit together, makes it all worth it.
